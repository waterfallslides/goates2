# 🎮 How to Use the Food System

## Controls

| Action | Control | Description |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **Collect Food** | Click on food or use proximity prompt | Pick up food from the ground |
| **Equip Slot 1** | Press `1` key | Equip food in first slot |
| **Equip Slot 2** | Press `2` key | Equip food in second slot |
| **Equip Slot 3** | Press `3` key | Equip food in third slot |
| **Consume Food** | Click anywhere (when holding food) | Eat the equipped food to restore health/hunger |
| **Unequip** | Press same number key again | Put food away |

---

## How It Works

### 1. Collecting Food
- During **daytime**, 30 food items spawn randomly across the map
- Walk up to food and **click** it or use the **proximity prompt** (E key)
- Food goes into your inventory (max 3 different types)

### 2. Equipping Food (Roblox-Style)
- Press **1**, **2**, or **3** to equip food from that slot
- The food appears **in your hand** as a Tool
- Green checkmark (✓) shows which slot is equipped

### 3. Consuming Food
- While holding food, **click anywhere** on screen
- Food is consumed and you gain:
  - ❤️ **Health** restored
  - 🍖 **Hunger** restored
- Food count decreases by 1

### 4. Day/Night Cycle
- **Day** (5 minutes): Food spawns, collect as much as you can!
- **Night** (3 minutes): All food disappears, survive on what you collected

✅ **Collect rare food first** - Cooked Meat is the best!
✅ **Use number keys** - Faster than clicking slots

## Troubleshooting

### "Can't collect food"
- Make sure inventory isn't full (3/3 different types)
- Check if you've reached stack limit for that food
- Food might be bugged - try different food item

### "Number keys don't work"
- Make sure you're not typing in chat
- Check that slot has food in it
- Try clicking the slot first

### "Food doesn't appear in hand"
- Wait 1 second after collecting
- Try pressing the number key again
- Check Output (F9) for errors

### "Click doesn't consume"
- Make sure you're holding the Tool (press 1/2/3)
- Food should be visible in your hand
- Try equipping again
